[germanua](https://github.com/germanua). This policy explains sections 4–6 of the
[license](../LICENSE); it does not create additional intellectual-property rights.

## Which assets are covered?

| Material | Treatment |
| --- | --- |
| Linux Soundboard name and distinctive project identity | Reference is allowed; misleading affiliation and use as another product's identity are restricted where the maintainer holds enforceable rights. |
| Application icon in `assets/icons/icon.png` and app-icon variants in `src/resources/icons/*/apps/` | Project artwork, subject to any applicable provenance or file-specific notice. Reference uses are described below. |
| Original screenshots in `assets/screenshots/` | May illustrate coverage of this project under the license's editorial permission. Third-party content visible in them keeps its own rights. |
| Symbolic toolbar, device, and folder glyphs | Some derive from Material Design, Feather, or Lucide. Their upstream licenses apply; see [third-party licenses](../THIRDPARTY_LICENSES.md#third-party-ui-assets). |
| Generic controls, audio concepts, and descriptive terminology | This policy does not reserve ordinary UI conventions, functionality, or descriptive language. |

The policy is not evidence of trademark registration or a complete provenance
certificate for every image. No registered-mark symbol is claimed. The project
does not claim ownership of the Linux name or third-party icon designs.

## References, tutorials, and reviews

You may name the app, show screenshots, and display its unaltered app icon when
identifying or discussing the official project. This includes independent
reviews, comparisons, tutorials, and bug reports, including monetized coverage.

- Identify the product accurately; link to the official project where practical.
- Keep the icon recognizable. Proportional resizing is fine.
- Crop or annotate screenshots to explain a point without misrepresenting the UI.
- Make your own identity clear. Do not imply the maintainer wrote, approved, or
  sponsors your content.

Criticism does not require endorsement. Statutory exceptions and lawful
nominative or descriptive use remain unaffected.

## Uses requiring written permission

Subject to applicable law and independent licenses, written permission is needed
before using protected project assets for another app, an unofficial installer,
a modified build, a reseller listing, a branded service, or merchandise.

Changing the app name does not authorize reuse of its code or artwork. Adding
"unofficial", a disclaimer, or attribution does not authorize redistribution.
Do not create lookalike store pages, update channels, domains, social accounts,
or package identities that mislead users about the product's origin.

A separately licensed fork needs both the relevant code rights and any branding
permission. These are distinct grants. Upstream icon reuse under its own license
does not grant permission to impersonate Linux Soundboard.

## Request permission or report impersonation

Use the [official project](https://github.com/germanua/Linux-SoundBoard) or the
contact channel published by its maintainer. Include the proposed use or the
suspected copy's URL, the affected asset, and relevant screenshots or revision
identifiers. Do not publish private personal data or access credentials.

Only the rights holder or an authorized representative can approve additional
rights. A request or a payment alone is not approval.
